# 14 没有中文输入法时的英文救命句

先发：

> Please answer in Chinese. Keep all engineering artifacts in English.

然后用非常短的英文。

---

## 选题 / Scope

- Compare these prompts for a 3-hour build. Do not code yet.
- Which one best fits my background?
- Scope this for 3 hours.
- Review my scope as a senior engineer.
- Too large? Cut aggressively.
- What is over-engineered?
- What should be out of scope?
- Give me the smallest meaningful vertical slice.
- What should I deploy first?

---

## 实现

- Plan first. Do not edit yet.
- Implement only this step.
- Do not expand scope.
- Keep it simple.
- Explain before editing.
- Review the diff.
- What changed and why?
- Can I explain all of this in code review?

---

## Debug

- Root cause first. Do not edit yet.
- What evidence supports this?
- Smallest fix only.
- Do not refactor unrelated code.
- Reproduce before fixing.
- Check logs first.
- What should I test after the fix?

---

## 时间管理

- I have 90 minutes left. Re-prioritize.
- I have 60 minutes left. Cut optional work.
- I have 30 minutes left. No new features.
- Should I deploy now?
- What should I stop doing?
- What is the single highest-value next step?

---

## Review

- Review as a senior engineer.
- What will a Tech Lead challenge?
- What are the production limitations?
- What breaks at 100x traffic?
- What should I discuss instead of implementing now?
- Prepare my code-review talking points.
